Pricing & payment

Pricing and payment

How much does a chauffeur cost in Northern Ireland?

Belfast City Airport to the city from £55; Belfast International from £75; Dublin Airport to Belfast from £220; hourly hire from £75 (3-hour minimum); full-day tours from £495 per vehicle. All per vehicle, S-Class or V-Class, fixed in advance. confirm Full pricing guide.

Is the price per person or per vehicle?

Per vehicle. Two people in an S-Class or six in a V-Class pay the same vehicle price.

Are there any hidden charges?

No. The quote is the price. Tolls and standard parking are included confirm, and there is no automatic night, weekend or bank-holiday surcharge confirm — if a different rate applies to your date it is on the quote before you book. Waiting beyond the allowance and additions to the itinerary are charged at the agreed hourly rate and always discussed first — what is included.

How do I pay?

Card, bank transfer, or monthly invoice on a business account. Sterling or euro confirm.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation more than 24 hours before a transfer, more than 7 days before a day tour, and more than 14 days before a multi-day programme or wedding. Inside those windows we charge 50%, rising to 100% under 12 hours (transfers), 72 hours (tours) or 7 days (multi-day). No-shows are charged in full. confirm Peak dates can differ and will say so on your quote — full cancellation terms.

Do you charge for waiting time at the airport?

You get 60 minutes free from the moment you actually land confirm — not from the booked time, because we track the flight and move the collection. Cruise and ferry pick-ups get 45 minutes from docking; any other address gets 15 minutes from the booked time. After that, waiting is charged in 15-minute units at the hourly rate on your quote. Your chauffeur calls and texts before the allowance runs out — waiting time in full.

Do you charge more at night, at weekends or on bank holidays?

No automatic surcharge confirm. Plenty of operators add one. We quote a single price, and if a different rate applies to your date it is shown on the quote before you book.

Do you take a deposit?

Not for a standard transfer. For day tours, multi-day programmes, weddings and peak dates we may ask for 25% confirm at the time of booking, refundable in full if you cancel inside the free-cancellation window.

Booking

Booking

How do I book?

Phone, email, WhatsApp or the quote form. You'll get a written confirmation with price, chauffeur name and vehicle.

How far ahead should I book?

Transfers: 24–48 hours ideally, same-day often possible. Tours, cruises, weddings and summer weekends: as early as you can.

Can I book for someone else?

Yes — most corporate bookings come from PAs, EAs, travel managers and agents.

Do you take travel agent and tour operator bookings?

Yes, including on account, and we can white-label for DMCs.

Do you operate 24/7?

Yes, by prior booking confirm.

Can I change my booking after it is confirmed?

Yes, and it is free if you tell us more than 24 hours before a transfer or more than 7 days before a tour, subject to availability. Later than that we will still try. Extra stops or extra time on the day are usually no problem and are charged at the hourly rate, agreed with you before the clock starts — changes and cancellations.

What happens if you have to cancel my booking?

We tell you as soon as we know, offer another vehicle or another time, and refund you in full if we cannot cover it. If a vehicle goes off the road we substitute one of the same or higher specification and tell you — if we change or cancel.

Airports

Airport transfers

Which airports do you cover?

All of them: Belfast International, Belfast City, Dublin, City of Derry, Shannon, Cork and Knock.

Where does the chauffeur meet me?

In the arrivals hall with a name board, unless you ask for kerbside.

What if my flight is delayed or cancelled?

Delayed: nothing for you to do. We track the flight number on your booking and move the collection to the actual landing time, at no charge. Cancelled: tell us the new flight and we move the booking to it free of charge, subject to a vehicle being available; if we cannot cover the new time we refund in full. We can only track a flight we have been given, so please supply the number when you book — delays and cancellations.

Is there a border stop between Dublin and Belfast?

No — no stop, no checks, no vehicle change.

Can you guarantee I will catch my flight?

No operator honestly can — we drive on public roads. What we do is plan the departure with a margin, watch the traffic on the day, and tell you the time we recommend leaving. Give us your check-in deadline when you book. If you ask to be collected later than we advise, the risk of a missed connection is yours — journey times and connections.

Tours

Tours

Are your drivers tour guides?

They know the routes and stories well and are praised for it; they are not accredited Blue Badge guides confirm. A qualified guide can be added.

Are admissions included?

No, unless a tour page says so. We'll tell you what to pre-book.

Can we change the itinerary on the day?

Yes — it's your tour.

Can tours start in Dublin or from a cruise ship?

Yes. Dublin adds about two hours each way; cruise excursions start and end at the Belfast terminal.

Vehicles

Vehicles

What vehicles do you have?

Mercedes-Benz S-Class (2), V-Class (6), Tourer Revolution (7) and Sprinter (16). See the fleet.

Do you provide child seats?

Yes, on request when booking.

Can you take golf clubs, bikes or lots of luggage?

Yes — tell us what you're bringing and we'll allocate the right vehicle.

Are the vehicles wheelchair accessible?

Confirm — tell us your needs and we'll advise honestly.

Can I bring a dog?

Assistance dogs travel at any time, at no charge and with no notice needed. Other animals are welcome by prior arrangement and need to be suitably contained; if the vehicle needs valeting afterwards a cleaning charge applies.

What happens if the vehicle is damaged or soiled?

Normal use is normal use. Where a vehicle needs specialist cleaning and comes off the road we charge a valeting fee of £150 confirm, and damage is charged at the cost of repair. We evidence any charge with photographs and the invoice — damage and cleaning.
