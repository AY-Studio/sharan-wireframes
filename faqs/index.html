Pricing & payment

Pricing and payment

How much does a chauffeur cost in Northern Ireland?

Belfast City Airport to the city from £55; Belfast International from £75; Dublin Airport to Belfast from £220; hourly hire from £75 (3-hour minimum); full-day tours from £495 per vehicle. All per vehicle, S-Class or V-Class, fixed in advance. confirm Full pricing guide.

Is the price per person or per vehicle?

Per vehicle. Two people in an S-Class or six in a V-Class pay the same vehicle price.

Are there any hidden charges?

No. The quote is the price. Tolls and parking are included unless stated confirm; waiting time beyond the agreed allowance and itinerary extensions are charged at the agreed hourly rate and always discussed first.

How do I pay?

Card, bank transfer, or monthly invoice on a business account. Sterling or euro confirm.

What is your cancellation policy?

Confirm policy — e.g. free cancellation up to 24 hours before a transfer and 7 days before a tour; later cancellations may be charged in full.

Do you charge for waiting time at the airport?

Reasonable waiting for delayed flights is included because we track the flight. Waiting caused by the passenger is charged after an allowance confirm.

Booking

Booking

How do I book?

Phone, email, WhatsApp or the quote form. You'll get a written confirmation with price, chauffeur name and vehicle.

How far ahead should I book?

Transfers: 24–48 hours ideally, same-day often possible. Tours, cruises, weddings and summer weekends: as early as you can.

Can I book for someone else?

Yes — most corporate bookings come from PAs, EAs, travel managers and agents.

Do you take travel agent and tour operator bookings?

Yes, including on account, and we can white-label for DMCs.

Do you operate 24/7?

Yes, by prior booking confirm.

Airports

Airport transfers

Which airports do you cover?

All of them: Belfast International, Belfast City, Dublin, City of Derry, Shannon, Cork and Knock.

Where does the chauffeur meet me?

In the arrivals hall with a name board, unless you ask for kerbside.

What if my flight is delayed or cancelled?

We track it and adjust. If it's cancelled, tell us the new flight and we rebook — no charge for the change confirm.

Is there a border stop between Dublin and Belfast?

No — no stop, no checks, no vehicle change.

Tours

Tours

Are your drivers tour guides?

They know the routes and stories well and are praised for it; they are not accredited Blue Badge guides confirm. A qualified guide can be added.

Are admissions included?

No, unless a tour page says so. We'll tell you what to pre-book.

Can we change the itinerary on the day?

Yes — it's your tour.

Can tours start in Dublin or from a cruise ship?

Yes. Dublin adds about two hours each way; cruise excursions start and end at the Belfast terminal.

Vehicles

Mercedes-Benz S-Class (2), V-Class (6), Tourer (7) and Sprinter (16). See the fleet.

Yes, on request when booking.

Can you take golf clubs, bikes or lots of luggage?

Yes — tell us what you're bringing and we'll allocate the right vehicle.

Are the vehicles wheelchair accessible?

Confirm — tell us your needs and we'll advise honestly.
