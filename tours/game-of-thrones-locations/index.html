The tour

What's included

Northern Ireland was the production's home for eight seasons and the locations are unusually close together. We run two versions: the Antrim Coast day (above), which doubles as a Causeway Coast tour, and a County Down day around Winterfell at Castle Ward, Tollymore Forest and Inch Abbey, near our chauffeurs' home ground. Fans can add the Game of Thrones Studio Tour at Banbridge (admission extra) to either.

FAQ

Questions about this tour

Can we include the Game of Thrones Studio Tour?

Yes — it's at Banbridge, about 40 minutes from Belfast, and takes 2–3 hours. Best combined with the County Down day.

Which version should we choose?

Antrim Coast if you want scenery and the Causeway too; County Down for Winterfell and fewer crowds. Or two days.

Do the chauffeurs know the show?

Well enough to point at a beach and name the scene. For a costumed superfan experience we can recommend specialist guides.

Are the locations walkable?

Most are short walks from the car park; Castle Ward and Tollymore involve a little more on foot.
