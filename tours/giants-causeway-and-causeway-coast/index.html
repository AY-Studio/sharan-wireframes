The tour

What's included

The Causeway Coast is the reason most visitors come to Northern Ireland, and it rewards a private car: the best light at the Dark Hedges is before the coaches arrive, Carrick-a-Rede needs a timed ticket, and the Causeway itself is far better at 3pm than 11am. Your chauffeur plans the order of stops around the day's conditions and crowds, so you spend the time at the cliffs rather than in a queue.

FAQ

Questions about this tour

Do I need to book Giant's Causeway tickets?

Walking to the stones is free. The visitor centre charges admission and is optional; National Trust members are free. We'll advise on the day.

Do I need to book Carrick-a-Rede?

Yes, in season it is timed-entry. We'll tell you which slot suits the itinerary; you book online, or we can arrange it.

Can we start from Dublin?

Yes. Dublin adds about two hours each way; many guests do the tour one way and finish in Belfast, or the reverse.

Can we add Bushmills Distillery?

Yes — a tour takes about an hour; book in advance in summer.

Is it suitable for children and older travellers?

Yes. The stops are flexible; the Causeway has a shuttle bus from the visitor centre to the stones for those who prefer not to walk down.
