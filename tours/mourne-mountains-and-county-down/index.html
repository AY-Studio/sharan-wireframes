The tour

What's included

County Down is fifty minutes from Belfast and most visitors never see it. This is our home patch: our chauffeurs know the back roads through the Mournes, which café in Newcastle is worth stopping at and how to time the Strangford ferry. It pairs well with a Royal County Down golf day, with the County Down Game of Thrones locations, or as a quieter alternative to the Causeway Coast.

FAQ

Questions about this tour

Is there much walking?

As little or as much as you like — Silent Valley and Tollymore have flat paths and longer trails.

Can we combine this with golf at Royal County Down?

Yes — a round in the morning and the tour in the afternoon, or the reverse. Golf transport.

Does this include Game of Thrones locations?

Castle Ward, Tollymore and Inch Abbey are on the route; say so and we'll lean into them.

Where does it start?

Belfast, Bangor, Newcastle or Dublin — the Mournes are on the way north from Dublin Airport.
