The tour

What's included

Belfast is a city best explained by someone who lives in it. In four hours you cover the industrial story at Titanic Quarter, the political story on the peace walls, and the civic city in between, with time for a pint or a coffee wherever your chauffeur recommends. Popular with cruise passengers, corporate guests with a free afternoon, and visitors with a day before heading north.

FAQ

Questions about this tour

Is Titanic Belfast admission included?

No — it's a timed-entry ticket, best pre-booked; we'll advise the slot that fits.

Is the murals tour political?

It's historical. Our chauffeurs explain both sides plainly and answer questions.

Can we do this from the cruise terminal?

Yes — it's the most popular half-day for ships with a short call. Cruise excursions.

Can we extend to the Causeway?

Yes — a combined 9–10 hour day is possible with an early start; ask for a quote.
