FAQ

Belfast questions

Where are you based in Belfast?

City East Business Centre, 68–72 Newtownards Road, BT4 1GW — East Belfast, near Titanic Quarter and City Airport. Bookings are by phone, email or the quote form rather than walk-in.

Do you do short journeys within Belfast?

Yes — hotel to office, restaurant to home. There is a minimum fare confirm; for several short journeys in a day, hourly hire is usually better value.

How far ahead do I need to book in Belfast?

Same-day is often possible; 24–48 hours is safer, and further ahead for tours and weekends.
