Corporate & executive travel

Executive transport that behaves like part of your team

Business travel fails at the edges: the car that is late, the driver who cannot find the entrance, the receipt that never arrives. SHARAN is built to remove those failures. Your chauffeur is briefed on the itinerary, monitors your flight, texts you the vehicle details before arrival and waits as long as it takes. In the car you get a quiet cabin, chargers, water and Wi-Fi confirm Wi-Fi, and a driver who does not make conversation unless you start it.

  • Named account contact who knows your regular passengers and preferences
  • Book by phone, email or WhatsApp — no app required
  • Consolidated monthly invoice with cost-centre or passenger references on each line
  • Fixed pre-agreed rates for your regular routes (e.g. office ↔ Dublin Airport)
  • Card, bank transfer and purchase-order payment confirm
  • Confirmation email for every booking with chauffeur name and vehicle registration on the day

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The questions we are asked most. See all 30 questions.

How much does a chauffeur cost in Northern Ireland?

Belfast City Airport to the city from £55; Belfast International from £75; Dublin Airport to Belfast from £220; hourly hire from £75 (3-hour minimum); full-day tours from £495 per vehicle. All per vehicle, S-Class or V-Class, fixed in advance. confirm Full pricing guide.

Is the price per person or per vehicle?

Per vehicle. Two people in an S-Class or six in a V-Class pay the same vehicle price.

Which airports do you cover?

All of them: Belfast International, Belfast City, Dublin, City of Derry, Shannon, Cork and Knock.

Where does the chauffeur meet me?

In the arrivals hall with a name board, unless you ask for kerbside.

Can I open a business account?

Yes. Business accounts.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — licensed for private hire in Northern Ireland and the Republic licence details and fully insured for hire and reward.
