What we do

Executive transport that behaves like part of your team

Business travel fails at the edges: the car that is late, the driver who cannot find the entrance, the receipt that never arrives. SHARAN is built to remove those failures. Your chauffeur is briefed on the itinerary, monitors your flight, texts you the vehicle details before arrival and waits as long as it takes. In the car you get a quiet cabin, chargers, water and Wi-Fi confirm Wi-Fi, and a driver who does not make conversation unless you start it.

Airport meet-and-greet

Chauffeur in arrivals with a name board at Belfast International, Belfast City or Dublin Airport. Flight tracked, waiting time built in.

Meeting-to-meeting transfers

Point-to-point across Belfast or Dublin, or a chauffeur on standby for the day so you are never waiting for a taxi.

Day and multi-day hire

A dedicated vehicle and chauffeur for roadshows, site visits, investor days and conferences anywhere in Ireland.

Client and VIP hospitality

Collect guests from the airport, take them to dinner, the Open, a distillery — and back to the hotel. We handle the logistics; you host.

Staff and contract runs

Regular scheduled journeys for senior staff, relocations or cross-border commutes, on a fixed contract rate.

Event and conference shuttles

Sprinter and Tourer vehicles for delegations, board visits and off-sites. Up to 16 passengers per vehicle.

Business accounts

Book once, invoice monthly

A SHARAN business account gives your office, PAs and travel bookers one number to call and one invoice at the end of the month.

  • Named account contact who knows your regular passengers and preferences
  • Book by phone, email or WhatsApp — no app required
  • Consolidated monthly invoice with cost-centre or passenger references on each line
  • Fixed pre-agreed rates for your regular routes (e.g. office ↔ Dublin Airport)
  • Card, bank transfer and purchase-order payment confirm
  • Confirmation email for every booking with chauffeur name and vehicle registration on the day

FAQ

Corporate travel questions

How is corporate chauffeur hire priced?

Point-to-point journeys are quoted as a fixed price, so the fare does not change if traffic is bad. Day hire is priced per hour from £75 confirm with a three-hour minimum, or as a full-day rate. Account clients get fixed rates for their regular routes.

Can my PA book on my behalf?

Yes. Most corporate bookings come from PAs, EAs and travel managers. We send confirmations to the booker and, if requested, the passenger.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We track every inbound flight and adjust the collection time automatically. You are not charged for waiting time caused by a flight delay confirm policy.

Do you provide receipts and VAT invoices?

Yes. Every journey is invoiced with VAT shown confirm VAT registration, either per job or consolidated monthly on account.

Are your chauffeurs licensed and vetted?

All chauffeurs hold the required taxi/PSV licensing for Northern Ireland and the Republic of Ireland confirm licence details, are fully insured for hire and reward, and are experienced in executive work.

Can you handle a multi-city roadshow?

Yes — Belfast, Dublin, Cork and Galway in one trip with the same chauffeur is a regular request. See roadshows and conferences.

Set up an account or book a single journey

Tell us the passenger, the dates and where they need to be. We confirm within business hours, usually within the hour.
