How it works

A dedicated chauffeur for the whole programme

Send us the draft schedule. We come back with a transport plan showing realistic travel times between each stop (including the Belfast–Dublin run at the time of day you'll be travelling), where the vehicle will wait, and a single price for the programme. On the day, your chauffeur has the itinerary, every venue contact number and the hotel details — changes on the fly are expected.

  • Investor and analyst roadshows: Belfast, Dublin, Cork, Galway in a single trip
  • Conference and exhibition shuttles between hotel, venue and dinners
  • Board and site visits for visiting parent-company executives
  • Inward-investment delegations hosted by agencies and councils
  • Film and TV production unit moves confirm if offered
  • Multi-vehicle convoys with a lead chauffeur coordinating

FAQ

Roadshow questions

How far ahead do you need the itinerary?

A draft a week out is ideal; final timings the day before are fine. We build in buffer for the Belfast–Dublin leg and airport check-in.

Can we make changes during the day?

Yes. Your chauffeur is on standby for the day and rerouting is part of the job. Additional hours are charged at the agreed hourly rate.

Do you provide more than one vehicle?

Yes. For larger delegations we run multiple vehicles with a lead chauffeur coordinating departures.

Do you cover the whole island in one trip?

Yes. Cross-border journeys are seamless with the same vehicle and driver.
