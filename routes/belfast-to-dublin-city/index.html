FAQ

Belfast to Dublin city questions

How long does it take to drive from Belfast to Dublin?

About 2 hours to the city centre in normal conditions. Rush hour around Dublin adds 20–30 minutes.

Is there a border check between Belfast and Dublin?

No. There is no stop, no checks and no vehicle change — the same car and chauffeur take you the whole way.

Is a chauffeur better than the Enterprise train?

For one person travelling city centre to city centre, the train is hard to beat. A chauffeur wins with two or more people, luggage, an early appointment, or a destination that is not beside a station.

Can you do the return the same day?

Yes — that is a common corporate booking. We wait or return for you at an agreed time; standby is charged hourly.
