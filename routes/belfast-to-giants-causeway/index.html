The route

Belfast to the Giant's Causeway by road

There are two ways to drive from Belfast to the Giant's Causeway. The fast one is the M2 and A26 through Ballymena, about 1 h 15. The good one is the Antrim Coast Road through Carnlough, Cushendun and Ballintoy, which takes closer to two hours and is one of the best coastal drives in Europe. A chauffeur means you can take the slow road out and the fast road home.

FAQ

Belfast to the Giant's Causeway questions

How long does it take to drive from Belfast to the Giant's Causeway?

About 1 h 15 on the direct route via the M2 and A26. The Antrim Coast Road takes closer to 2 hours and is far more scenic.

How much is a taxi from Belfast to the Giant's Causeway?

A metered taxi will vary with traffic and whether the driver waits. A SHARAN transfer is from £140 per vehicle one way confirm, fixed before you travel.

Can we stop at the Dark Hedges and Carrick-a-Rede?

Yes. Once the vehicle is yours for the journey the stops are up to you. If you want the full day with all the stops, that is our Causeway Coast tour rather than a transfer.

Is there parking at the Giant's Causeway?

Yes, at the visitor centre, but it fills in summer. Being dropped at the door and collected later removes the problem entirely.
