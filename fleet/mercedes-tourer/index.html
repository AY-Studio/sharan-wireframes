Capacity

At a glance

The Tourer Revolution takes group transport to a more exclusive level, offering the space of a larger vehicle with the atmosphere of a private executive lounge. Its premium interior and considered passenger environment make longer journeys comfortable, relaxed and personal — the right choice for executive delegations, corporate hospitality days, VIP touring parties and family groups who want more room than a V-Class without stepping up to a sixteen-seater.
