How it works

What an airport transfer with SHARAN looks like

  • Before you fly — confirmation with chauffeur name, mobile number and vehicle. We ask for your flight number and track it.
  • On landing — your chauffeur is in the arrivals hall with a name board (or waits at the kerb for domestic flights if you prefer). Waiting time for delayed flights is covered confirm allowance.
  • The journey — a clean, quiet Mercedes with water and chargers. Your chauffeur handles luggage and the route.
  • Departures — we pick you up at a time calculated from your flight, terminal and the traffic that morning, not a guess.

FAQ

Airport transfer questions

How much is an airport transfer from Belfast?

Belfast City Airport to the city centre from £55; Belfast International from £75; Dublin Airport to Belfast from £220 — all in an S-Class, per vehicle not per person, fixed in advance. Larger vehicles are quoted on request. confirm all prices

Where will my chauffeur meet me?

In the arrivals hall holding a board with your name, unless you ask for a kerbside collection. You will have their mobile number in advance.

What if my flight is early or late?

We track the flight and move the pick-up. Reasonable waiting time for delays is included.

Do you do early morning and late night transfers?

Yes, 24 hours a day, seven days a week, by prior booking confirm 24/7.

Can you carry golf clubs, skis or lots of luggage?

Yes. The V-Class takes six large cases plus hand luggage; the Sprinter takes more. Tell us what you're bringing when you book.

Do you provide child seats?

Yes, on request at booking — infant, child and booster seats confirm.
