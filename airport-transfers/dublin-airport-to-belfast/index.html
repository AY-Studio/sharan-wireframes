How it works

Booking a Dublin Airport to Belfast transfer

  • Send your flight number, arrival date, number of passengers, luggage and the Belfast address (or hotel).
  • We confirm a fixed price and, the day before, your chauffeur's name, mobile and vehicle.
  • We track your flight. Your chauffeur is in arrivals when you land, whatever time that turns out to be.
  • Two hours later you are at your door in Belfast — or Bangor, Newcastle, Lisburn, Antrim: anywhere in the north.

FAQ

Dublin Airport to Belfast — common questions

How long does it take to get from Dublin Airport to Belfast?

About two hours by car via the M1 and A1. Early morning and late evening are quicker; weekday rush hour around Dublin and Belfast can add 20–30 minutes.

How much is a taxi from Dublin Airport to Belfast?

A metered airport taxi is typically €250–350 if the driver accepts the fare. A SHARAN private transfer is from £220 per vehicle, fixed in advance, with your chauffeur waiting in arrivals. confirm

Is there a border check between Dublin and Belfast?

No. There is no stop or checkpoint on the road between the Republic of Ireland and Northern Ireland.

Can I pay in euro?

Yes — we quote in sterling or euro, whichever you prefer confirm.

Can we stop on the way?

Yes. A comfort stop is no problem; longer detours (Newgrange, the Mournes) can be added to the quote.

Do you also do Belfast to Dublin Airport?

Yes, at the same rate. We calculate the pick-up time from your flight and the traffic that morning.
