The service

Chauffeur transfers to and from Belfast International Airport

Belfast International sits at Aldergrove, about 18 miles north-west of the city. It is the busier of Belfast's two airports for holiday and European flights, and the one where a pre-booked chauffeur saves the most stress: taxi queues at peak arrival times are long, and public transport into the city means a bus and a walk with luggage. Your SHARAN chauffeur is in the arrivals hall with a name board, has your flight tracked, and takes you directly to your door.

FAQ

Belfast International Airport transfer questions

How much is a chauffeur from Belfast International Airport to Belfast?

From £75 to Belfast city centre in an S-Class, per vehicle, fixed in advance. Larger vehicles and onward destinations are quoted on request. confirm

Is the price per person?

No — per vehicle. Up to two passengers in the S-Class, six in the V-Class, at the same vehicle price.

What if my flight lands early?

We track it. Your chauffeur adjusts and is there when you walk out.

Can you take us onward somewhere other than Belfast?

Yes — anywhere in Ireland. Newcastle, Newry, Derry, Enniskillen, the Causeway Coast, Donegal: give us the address and we quote door to door.
