The service

Chauffeur transfers to and from Dublin Airport

Dublin Airport is where most long-haul visitors to Northern Ireland actually land. The road north is good (M1 to Belfast in about two hours) but the options for getting there with luggage are poor: the coach is slow with a change, and taxis frequently decline cross-border fares. A pre-booked chauffeur fixes this. We meet you in the arrivals hall of your terminal, and the price is agreed before you fly — in sterling or euro confirm currency handling.

FAQ

Dublin Airport transfer questions

How much is a chauffeur from Dublin Airport to Belfast?

From £220 to Belfast in an S-Class, per vehicle, fixed in advance. Larger vehicles and onward destinations are quoted on request. confirm

Is the price per person?

No — per vehicle. Up to two passengers in the S-Class, six in the V-Class, at the same vehicle price.

What if my flight lands early?

We track it. Your chauffeur adjusts and is there when you walk out.

Can you take us onward somewhere other than Belfast?

Yes — anywhere in Ireland. Newcastle, Newry, Derry, Enniskillen, the Causeway Coast, Donegal: give us the address and we quote door to door.
