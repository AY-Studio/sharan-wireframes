The service

Chauffeur transfers to and from Belfast City Airport

George Best Belfast City Airport handles most UK domestic and business routes — London, Manchester, Edinburgh, Birmingham — which makes it the airport our corporate clients use most. It is compact, so a kerbside collection is often the quickest option; for visiting guests we meet in arrivals with a name board. Being based a few minutes away on the Newtownards Road means we are rarely far from the terminal.

FAQ

Belfast City Airport transfer questions

How much is a chauffeur from Belfast City Airport to Belfast?

From £55 to Belfast city centre in an S-Class, per vehicle, fixed in advance. Larger vehicles and onward destinations are quoted on request. confirm

Is the price per person?

No — per vehicle. Up to two passengers in the S-Class, six in the V-Class, at the same vehicle price.

What if my flight lands early?

We track it. Your chauffeur adjusts and is there when you walk out.

Can you take us onward somewhere other than Belfast?

Yes — anywhere in Ireland. Newcastle, Newry, Derry, Enniskillen, the Causeway Coast, Donegal: give us the address and we quote door to door.
