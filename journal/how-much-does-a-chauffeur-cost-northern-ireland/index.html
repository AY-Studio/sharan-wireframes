How much does a chauffeur cost in Northern Ireland?

Short answer: from £55 for a short airport transfer, £75 an hour for hourly hire, £220 for Dublin Airport to Belfast and £495 for a full-day tour — all per vehicle, not per person. Here's the detail and what moves the number.

What changes the price

  • Vehicle. S-Class and V-Class are the baseline; the seven-seat Tourer Revolution and sixteen-seat Sprinter cost more per journey but far less per head.
  • Distance and positioning. A journey that starts far from Belfast includes the chauffeur getting there; a return trip is cheaper per leg than two singles.
  • Waiting and hours. Airport waiting for a delayed flight is included; standby time between meetings is charged hourly.
  • Time of day. Some operators add night and bank-holiday surcharges. SHARAN does not confirm — the quoted price is the price, and any different rate is shown before you book.
  • Admissions and extras. Tour prices cover the vehicle and chauffeur, not tickets or meals.

Chauffeur vs taxi vs app: is it worth it?

For a solo short hop across Belfast, a taxi is cheaper. A chauffeur wins when any of these apply: two or more people (the price is per vehicle), luggage or golf clubs, an early flight you cannot miss, a cross-border journey, a visiting client you want to impress, or a full day where you'd otherwise be booking five taxis. Dublin Airport to Belfast is the clearest case — a metered taxi is often €250–350 if the driver accepts the fare, against a fixed £220 with the chauffeur waiting in arrivals.

How to compare quotes

  • Ask whether the price is per vehicle and fixed in advance
  • Ask what happens if the flight is late
  • Ask for the vehicle model and year, not 'luxury vehicle'
  • Check licensing and insurance for hire and reward
  • Read reviews that name the driver — that's the tell
