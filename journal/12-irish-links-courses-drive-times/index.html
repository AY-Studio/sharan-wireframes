12 Irish links courses and how far they are to drive

Driving times to twelve of the best links courses in Ireland from Belfast and Dublin Airport — and how to group them so a golf trip is not mostly car.

Ireland has more of the world's great links courses than anywhere its size has any right to, and they are not conveniently arranged. The single biggest mistake on a first golf trip is treating the map as flat: Royal County Down and Royal Portrush look close together and are two hours apart, and the Donegal courses are further from Belfast than the numbers suggest because the last half hour is single-track coast road.

Here are twelve courses we drive to regularly, with honest driving times from Belfast city centre and from Dublin Airport — the two places most trips actually start.

How to group them

Three clusters do most of the work, and a trip built around clusters spends far less of itself in the car.

  • County Down. Royal County Down and Ardglass are twenty minutes apart, with the Slieve Donard next door to the first tee. Two days here is easy.
  • The north coast. Royal Portrush, Portstewart and Castlerock are all within twenty minutes of each other. Two or three days, with the Causeway on a rest day.
  • Donegal. Portsalon, Rosapenna and Ballyliffin over two or three days. Cross-border, slower roads, and the best value golf on the island.

The two Dublin courses, Portmarnock and The Island, are twenty minutes from Dublin Airport and make a natural first or last round if you are flying through Dublin rather than Belfast.

Where the time actually goes

  • Belfast to the north coast: 1 h 15 each way. Fine daily; better to move hotel.
  • County Down to the north coast: about 2 hours. Move hotel rather than commute.
  • Anywhere to Donegal: add 30 minutes to whatever the map says. The last stretch is slow.
  • Dublin Airport to the north coast: 3 hours. Fly into Belfast International instead if you can.

Getting between them

This is the part we do. One chauffeur and one vehicle for the trip means clubs stay loaded, nobody navigates a single-track road in the rain, and the airport run at the end is somebody else's problem. Bags and passengers are the constraint: four players with clubs and cases is a V-Class; six needs a Tourer Revolution; a society takes a Sprinter.

FAQ

Common questions

Which Irish links courses are closest to Belfast?

Galgorm Castle at 35 minutes, Royal County Down at 50 and Ardglass at 55. Royal Portrush and Portstewart are both about 1 h 15.

Can you play Royal County Down and Royal Portrush in one trip?

Yes, but not comfortably in one day — they are about two hours apart. Most groups do County Down for two days, then move to the north coast for two.

How far is Royal Portrush from Dublin Airport?

About 3 hours. If your trip is mainly north-coast golf, flying into Belfast International saves roughly two hours each way.

Is it worth a chauffeur for a golf trip?

It depends on the shape of the trip. For one round near your hotel, no. For four rounds across two counties with clubs, cases and an early flight home, it is usually cheaper than hiring two cars and nobody has to skip the bar.
