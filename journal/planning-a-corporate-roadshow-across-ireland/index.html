Planning a corporate roadshow across Ireland

Ireland is small enough to cover in a week and large enough to get wrong. Here are the drive times that actually hold, the airports that make sense for each leg, and what to send your transport provider.

Pick airports by leg, not habit

  • Arriving from London for Belfast meetings: Belfast City (10 minutes from the centre) beats Belfast International and both beat Dublin.
  • Arriving long-haul: Dublin, then a chauffeur north — two hours to Belfast.
  • Departing after Galway or Limerick: Shannon. After Cork: Cork Airport. Don't drive back to Dublin for a flight if you don't have to.

One vehicle and chauffeur for the programme

Multi-city trips work best with one dedicated vehicle and chauffeur for the whole programme rather than a local car in each city: the itinerary lives with one person, changes are absorbed on the fly, luggage stays in the vehicle, and the price is one number. How SHARAN runs roadshows.

What to send your transport provider

  • Passenger names and mobile numbers (for the chauffeur, not marketing)
  • Flight numbers for every arrival and departure
  • Every venue address with a contact number and any parking/security instructions
  • Hotel for each night
  • Meeting start and end times — with honest slippage
  • Dietary or accessibility needs
  • Who signs off changes on the day

Mistakes we see

  • Booking a 9am Dublin meeting after an 8am Belfast breakfast
  • Assuming a taxi at Dublin Airport will accept a Belfast fare
  • Forgetting that Friday-afternoon Dublin traffic is a different city
  • Booking the S-Class for four people with luggage — take the V-Class
  • No buffer before the departing flight
