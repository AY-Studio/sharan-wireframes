1. Who we are

SHARAN Executive Transport ("we", "us", "our") is a chauffeur and private-hire operator based at City East Business Centre, 68–72 Newtownards Road, Belfast BT4 1GW. We are licensed for private hire in Northern Ireland and the Republic of Ireland licence numbers and insured for hire and reward confirm.

"You" means the person or organisation making the booking. Where a booking is made on someone else's behalf — by a PA, travel manager, agent or tour operator — the person making the booking is responsible for payment and for passing these terms to the passengers.

Contact: +44 7709 889608 · info@sharantransport.com

2. How we apply these terms

We would rather keep a client than win an argument. Where a delay or a change is genuinely outside your control and you tell us as soon as you know, we will normally waive a charge, and we will always look for a way to move a booking rather than cancel it.

These terms exist for the cases where we have held a vehicle and a chauffeur for you, turned other work away, and cannot recover that time. In those cases we reserve the right to apply the charges below in full.

3. Making a booking

A quotation is not a booking. A contract is formed only when we send you a written confirmation — by email or WhatsApp — setting out the price, the vehicle, the pick-up point and the time. Verbal and telephone bookings are always followed by a written confirmation.

Please check that confirmation as soon as you receive it and tell us immediately if anything is wrong. We work from the details on the confirmation.

Quotations are valid for 14 days confirm unless the quotation says otherwise, and are subject to vehicle availability at the time you accept.

4. Prices and what is included

Prices are quoted per vehicle, not per person, and are fixed at confirmation. The price does not change because traffic was bad or the route was longer than expected.

Included: the chauffeur, the vehicle, fuel, road tolls, standard parking at the pick-up and drop-off, the waiting allowance set out in clause 6, and VAT where it applies confirm VAT registration.

Not included: attraction admissions, meals, ferry fares, extended airport car-park charges confirm, waiting beyond the allowance, additions to the itinerary made on the day, and anything your quotation lists as excluded.

We do not apply an automatic night, weekend or bank-holiday surcharge confirm policy. If a different rate applies to your journey it is shown on your quotation before you book.

Prices are in pounds sterling. Euro payment is accepted at the rate shown on your quotation confirm.

Where an additional charge may become due, we will tell you before we apply it wherever it is practical to do so.

5. Payment

Private clients: payment by card or bank transfer, before or on the day of travel confirm.

Account clients: consolidated monthly invoice, payable within 30 days of the invoice date confirm. We may suspend an account that falls overdue, and we may charge interest and recovery costs on overdue business invoices under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998.

Deposits: for day tours, multi-day programmes, weddings and peak-date bookings we may ask for a deposit of 25% confirm at the time of booking. The deposit is refundable in full if you cancel inside the free-cancellation window in clause 8.

6. Waiting time

Every booking includes a free waiting allowance. It starts at different points depending on where we are collecting you.

Pick-upFree waitingStarts from
Airport arrivals60 minutes confirmthe actual landing time, not the booked time
Cruise terminal or ferry port45 minutes confirmthe actual docking time
Hotel, home, office or any other address15 minutes confirmthe booked pick-up time

Because we track your inbound flight and re-time the collection to the actual landing, a delayed flight does not eat into your free waiting — the allowance begins when you actually land. The same applies to a late ship.

Beyond the allowance, waiting is charged in 15-minute units at the hourly rate shown on your quotation, currently from £75 per hour confirm. Standby time booked between meetings is charged hourly from the start, not after an allowance.

Your chauffeur will call and text before the allowance runs out. We do not leave without trying to reach you.

7. Delayed or cancelled flights, ferries and ships

If your flight is delayed: nothing for you to do. We track the flight number on your booking and move the collection to match. There is no charge for the delay and no charge for the re-timing.

If your flight is cancelled: tell us the new flight as soon as you know. We will move your booking to it at no charge, subject to a vehicle being available. If we cannot cover the new time, we will refund you in full.

If you do not tell us: if the chauffeur travels to the airport for a flight you are no longer on, and we cannot reach you, clause 9 (no-shows) applies.

We can only track a flight we have been given. Please supply the flight number at the time of booking, and tell us if it changes.

8. Changing or cancelling your booking

Changes

Changes are free, subject to availability, if you tell us more than 24 hours before a transfer or more than 7 days before a tour or multi-day programme. Later than that we will still do our best; but where the change means we lose the vehicle for other work, the cancellation charges below may apply to the original booking.

Extra stops or extra time added on the day are usually no problem and are charged at the hourly rate on your quotation. Your chauffeur will agree it with you before the clock starts. If your passenger numbers increase beyond the vehicle's capacity we will need to allocate a larger vehicle, and the price will change.

Cancel by phone, email or WhatsApp. A cancellation takes effect when we acknowledge it, so please make sure you have our reply. Any refund due is paid within 14 days to the original payment method.

9. No-shows

If you do not arrive within the free waiting allowance and we cannot reach you on the number given at booking, the booking is treated as a no-show and charged in full, together with any waiting time already incurred.

Before we treat a booking as a no-show, your chauffeur will call and text the number on the booking, and at an airport will check the arrivals information and ask at the information desk.

10. If we change or cancel

We may substitute a different vehicle of equivalent or higher specification — for example if a vehicle is off the road for repair. We will tell you if that happens.

If we have to cancel, we will tell you as soon as we can, offer an alternative vehicle or time, and refund you in full if we cannot cover the booking. Except where clause 17 provides otherwise, that refund is the limit of our liability for a cancellation by us.

We may subcontract a booking to another licensed operator of equivalent standard. We remain responsible to you for the service.

11. Events outside our control

Neither of us is liable for a failure or delay caused by something outside our reasonable control. That includes severe weather, flood, snow and ice, road closures, accidents and exceptional congestion, industrial action, civil unrest, acts of government, epidemic or pandemic restrictions, fuel shortage, and mechanical breakdown despite proper maintenance.

Where one of these events affects your booking we will contact you as soon as we can and either rearrange the journey or refund it. We will not charge you a cancellation fee for an event of this kind.

12. Journey times and connections

We plan every airport, ferry and tee-time journey with a margin built in, we monitor traffic on the day, and our chauffeurs know the routes. But we travel on public roads and we cannot guarantee arrival at a specific time.

If you are travelling to a flight, ferry, sailing, train or tee time, tell us the deadline when you book and we will recommend a departure time. If you ask us to collect you later than we recommend, we are not responsible for a connection missed as a result.

13. Passengers, luggage and child seats

Passenger numbers must not exceed the number of seat belts in the vehicle. We cannot carry additional passengers, and we will refuse to do so.

Luggage capacities are listed for each vehicle on our fleet pages. Tell us what you are bringing when you book — in particular golf clubs, ski bags, bicycles, instruments and oversize items. We may refuse items that cannot be carried safely or that exceed the capacity you booked. Luggage is carried at your risk, subject to clause 17.

Child seats are provided free on request when you book confirm. We need the child's age and height to supply the right seat. Where a suitable seat has not been requested in advance, children may travel in the rear of a licensed private-hire vehicle under the exemption that applies to our licence, but we strongly recommend booking a seat.

Assistance dogs are always carried, at no charge and with no notice required. Other animals are carried by prior arrangement and must be suitably contained; a cleaning charge under clause 15 may apply.

14. Conduct in the vehicle

Smoking and vaping are prohibited in our vehicles by law. Seat belts must be worn by every passenger. Alcohol may be consumed in the vehicle only by prior agreement confirm.

Your chauffeur may refuse to begin, or may end, a journey where a passenger is abusive or threatening, is behaving unsafely or unlawfully, or where the chauffeur reasonably believes there is a risk to safety. Where that happens the full fare remains payable.

Our chauffeurs are bound by legal driving-hours and rest requirements. Where an itinerary would breach them we will arrange a second chauffeur, and will tell you in advance if that changes the price.

15. Damage and cleaning

You are responsible for damage or soiling caused by you or your party beyond normal use. Where a vehicle needs specialist cleaning and is taken off the road, we charge a valeting fee of £150 confirm. Damage is charged at the cost of repair, plus the income lost while the vehicle is unavailable confirm.

We will evidence any charge of this kind with photographs and the repair or valeting invoice.

16. Tours and shore excursions

Published itineraries are indicative. The order, timing and choice of stops may change on the day for weather, crowds, road conditions, attraction closures or — most often — because you would rather do something else. That flexibility is the point of a private tour.

Admissions, meals and accredited guides are not included unless your quotation says so. Our chauffeurs know the routes and the stories well but are not accredited Blue Badge guides confirm; a qualified guide can be arranged at extra cost.

Poor weather is not in itself grounds for a free cancellation once the cancellation ladder in clause 8 applies, but we will rearrange the date wherever we reasonably can.

Shore excursions are timed to your ship's published schedule with a return margin. Where a delay caused by us means you miss the ship, we will get you to the next port of call at our cost confirm. That undertaking does not apply where the delay was outside our control, or where you extended the itinerary against your chauffeur's advice.

17. Our liability

Nothing in these terms limits or excludes our liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or for anything else that cannot lawfully be limited or excluded.

Subject to that:

  • we are liable for loss that is a foreseeable result of our breaking this contract or failing to use reasonable care and skill;
  • we are not liable for loss that was not foreseeable at the time the contract was made;
  • where you are booking in the course of a business, we are not liable for loss of profit, loss of business, loss of contracts, loss of revenue or loss of anticipated savings; and
  • our total liability in connection with any one booking is limited to the greater of the price of that booking and £1,000 confirm.

We hold hire-and-reward motor insurance and public liability insurance confirm cover and limits.

18. Lost property

Vehicles are checked between jobs. Property found is logged and held for 30 days confirm. We will return an item by tracked courier at your cost, or hold it for collection from our Belfast office.

Subject to clause 17, we are not liable for property left in a vehicle.

19. Complaints

If something goes wrong, tell us within 14 days of the journey — by email to info@sharantransport.com or by phone on +44 7709 889608. We will acknowledge within 2 working days and give you a substantive response within 10 working days confirm.

20. Your data

We use your details only to deliver and invoice the service, and we use flight numbers only to track the flight for your collection. Full detail is in our privacy policy.

21. Your rights as a consumer

If you are booking as a consumer, your statutory rights are unaffected by these terms. We must supply the service with reasonable care and skill under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, and equivalent protections apply in the Republic of Ireland.

Please note that the 14-day right to cancel a distance contract does not apply to passenger transport services: they are excluded by regulation 6(1)(b) of the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, and by the equivalent Irish provision confirm with solicitor. The cancellation terms that apply to your booking are those in clause 8.

22. General

These terms and your written confirmation are the whole agreement between us. If any clause is found to be unenforceable, the remaining clauses continue in force. A person who is not a party to the contract has no right to enforce it.

We may update these terms. The version that applies to your booking is the version published when we sent your written confirmation.

23. Governing law

These terms are governed by the law of Northern Ireland, and the courts of Northern Ireland have jurisdiction. If you are a consumer resident in the Republic of Ireland, you keep the protection of the mandatory consumer law of Ireland and may bring proceedings in the Irish courts.
