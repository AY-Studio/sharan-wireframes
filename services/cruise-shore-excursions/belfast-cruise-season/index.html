The season

When the ships call, and what fits in the day

Belfast's cruise season runs from around April to October, with the busiest months in early summer and early autumn confirm with Belfast Harbour. Ships berth at Stormont Wharf confirm berth, and most calls give you eight to ten hours ashore.

That number is the only one that really matters. Everything below is built backwards from your all-aboard time, with a margin.

How we time it

Working backwards from all-aboard

  • We take your all-aboard time and build the day backwards from it, with a margin
  • The port schedule is monitored on the day; a late docking moves the start, not the cost
  • Your chauffeur carries the ship's agent contact and the terminal's number
  • Groups from several cabins can share a vehicle — the price is per vehicle, not per person

FAQ

Cruise season questions

When is the Belfast cruise season?

Broadly April to October, with the heaviest months in May, June, August and September confirm with Belfast Harbour.

How long do ships stay in Belfast?

Most calls are eight to ten hours alongside confirm. That is comfortably enough for the Causeway Coast if you leave promptly, and very comfortable for Belfast and County Down.

What fits in a day ashore?

From an eight-hour call: the Giant's Causeway and the Causeway Coast, or Belfast and Titanic, or the Mournes and County Down. All three are full days — we would not try to combine them.

What happens if the ship is late docking?

You are not charged for waiting caused by the ship. We monitor the port schedule and adjust the start.

What if we are delayed and miss the ship?

Where a delay is our fault we get you to the next port of call at our cost confirm. That undertaking does not apply where the delay is outside our control — see our tour and excursion terms.
