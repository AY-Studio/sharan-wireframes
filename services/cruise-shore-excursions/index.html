Port logistics

How a private excursion from Belfast port works

  • Cruise ships berth at Belfast Harbour (Stormont Wharf or Pollock Dock, about 10 minutes from the city centre). Your chauffeur meets you at the terminal exit with a name board — we confirm the exact meeting point once your ship's berth is published.
  • We work from your ship's docking and all-aboard times and build the day backwards from all-aboard, with a buffer.
  • Your itinerary is yours: we suggest, you decide. Stay longer where you like it, skip what you don't.
  • Back-to-ship guarantee: if a delay on our side means you miss the ship, we get you to the next port confirm policy.
  • Price is per vehicle, not per person — a family of six in a V-Class often pays less than six coach tickets.

FAQ

Cruise excursion questions

Where exactly do you meet us at Belfast cruise port?

At the terminal exit of whichever berth your ship is allocated — usually Stormont Wharf. We confirm the meeting point and your chauffeur's mobile number the day before.

Is there enough time to get to the Giant's Causeway and back?

Yes, with a typical 8–10 hour port call. The Causeway is about 75 minutes each way, leaving four to five hours on the coast.

What if the ship is late docking?

We monitor the port schedule and adjust. You are not charged for waiting caused by the ship.

Do you guarantee we'll be back on time?

We plan the day backwards from all-aboard with a buffer and our chauffeurs know the traffic. See our back-to-ship policy confirm.

Can we book a group larger than six?

Yes — the Tourer takes seven and the Sprinter sixteen, or we run two vehicles.
