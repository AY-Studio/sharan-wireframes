Championship weeks

Getting to Royal Portrush when the world turns up

Royal Portrush hosted The Open in 2019 and again in 2025, and the Irish Open returns to the north coast regularly. A championship week is a completely different transport problem from an ordinary round: roads close, park-and-ride replaces normal parking, and the drop-off points are set by the organisers rather than by the club.

We plan around the published traffic management plan for that year confirm dates and plan each year, not around a normal day's driving.

The week

What changes during a championship

  • Road closures and one-way systems around Portrush from several days before
  • Park-and-ride from designated sites; normal club parking is unavailable
  • Drop-off and pick-up points are fixed by the organisers and move year to year confirm
  • Finishing times move with the weather — a fixed collection time rarely survives contact
  • Accommodation on the north coast sells out years in advance; most groups base in Belfast

How we work it

Our approach to the week

  • One chauffeur and one vehicle for the whole week where possible, so nothing is re-explained each morning
  • A collection window rather than a fixed time, with the chauffeur in contact by text
  • Non-golfers dropped at the Causeway, Bushmills or Portstewart while play is on
  • Hospitality groups moved by Tourer Revolution or Sprinter, with the same driver each day

FAQ

Championship week questions

Where can a car drop off at Royal Portrush during The Open?

Drop-off points move each championship and are set by the organisers and the PSNI in the weeks before. We work to the published plan for that year confirm each year and tell you the exact point the night before.

Is a chauffeur better than park-and-ride?

For a group, usually yes. Park-and-ride means a shuttle queue at both ends of a long day. A chauffeur drops you as close as the traffic plan allows and collects you at an agreed point and time.

Can you collect us after play finishes?

Yes. Finishing times move with the weather and the leaderboard, so we hold a collection window rather than a fixed minute and your chauffeur stays in contact by text.

Do you take corporate hospitality groups?

Yes — Sprinter and Tourer Revolution vehicles for hospitality parties, with the same chauffeur across the week. See corporate travel.

Should we stay in Portrush or Belfast?

Portrush and Portstewart accommodation sells out years ahead for a championship. Most groups end up in Belfast or the Causeway villages; from Belfast it is 1 h 15 each way, which is very manageable with a chauffeur but a long day by public transport.
