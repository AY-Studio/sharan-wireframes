The journey

Getting to Royal County Down

Royal County Down sits at the foot of the Mourne Mountains in Newcastle, about 50 minutes from Belfast and under two hours from Dublin Airport. It is the course most visiting golfers build a trip around, and tee times are hard-won — which is exactly why the transport should not be the thing that goes wrong.

On the day

How a day at Royal County Down works

  • The Championship (Annesley) links and the shorter Annesley course share the same entrance off the Downs Road
  • Bag drop is at the clubhouse; your chauffeur unloads and parks, and is back before you finish
  • The Slieve Donard is a two-minute drive — most groups stay there and we shuttle between the two
  • Newcastle is 20 minutes from Ardglass, so the two pair naturally into one day

FAQ

Royal County Down transport questions

How long is the drive from Belfast to Royal County Down?

About 50 minutes door to door, or an hour from Belfast International. From Dublin Airport allow 1 h 50.

Can you collect us from the Slieve Donard?

Yes — it is a two-minute run to the clubhouse. Most groups staying in Newcastle use us for the airport legs and the away days rather than the hop to the first tee.

Will the chauffeur wait during the round?

That is your call. On a single round most groups book a drop and a collection; on a two-course day we stay with you. Waiting inside the agreed allowance is included — see our waiting time terms.

Can you carry clubs for four players?

Four players with bags and cases is a V-Class. Beyond that we use a Tourer Revolution or Sprinter — tell us the bag count when you book.
