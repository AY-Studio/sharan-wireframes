The service

Built around the itinerary, not the car

Golf trips in Ireland involve early starts, long coastal drives and a lot of luggage. We plan the transport around your tee times: collection from Dublin or Belfast airport, transfers between hotels and courses each day, a stop at a distillery or the Causeway on a rest day, and the airport run home. The same chauffeur stays with the group for the trip, so nobody is waiting on a taxi in a car park at Portrush.

FAQ

Golf transport questions

How many golfers and bags fit in the V-Class?

Four golfers with four golf bags and four suitcases is the comfortable configuration. Six passengers fit if you are travelling without clubs or with clubs in a second vehicle.

Can you do a full golf trip — several days, several courses?

Yes. That is most of our golf work. One chauffeur and vehicle for the trip, priced as a package, with the airport runs at each end.

Do you know the courses?

Yes — our chauffeurs drive to Royal County Down, Royal Portrush and the Donegal links regularly and know the drop-off points and the pro-shop timings.

Can we combine golf with sightseeing?

Yes. The Causeway Coast courses sit next to the Giant's Causeway and Bushmills; a rest-day tour is easy to add. See the Causeway Coast tour.

Do you work with tour operators?

Yes — we take agent, DMC and tour-operator bookings and can invoice on account.
