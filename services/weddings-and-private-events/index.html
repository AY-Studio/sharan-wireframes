Weddings

The couple, the party and the guests — one coordinated plan

Wedding transport goes wrong when the bridal car is beautiful and the guests are stranded. We plan the whole day: an S-Class for the couple, V-Class for the bridal party and parents, and Sprinter shuttles between hotel, ceremony and venue on a timed loop. One chauffeur coordinates the timings with your planner or venue.

  • Bridal car: Mercedes-Benz S-Class, ribboned and presented confirm ribbons/decoration
  • Bridal party and parents: Mercedes-Benz V-Class or Tourer
  • Guest shuttles: Mercedes-Benz Sprinter, 16 seats, timed loops
  • Late-night returns to hotels and city centre
  • Next-day honeymoon run to Belfast or Dublin Airport
  • Chauffeurs assist photographers with vehicle positioning and timings

FAQ

Wedding and event questions

Do you have classic or vintage wedding cars?

No — our fleet is modern Mercedes-Benz. Couples choose us for a contemporary look and, especially, for moving the whole party and guests reliably.

How far ahead should we book?

As early as you have a date. Summer Saturdays go first.

Can you do guest shuttles between the hotel and the venue?

Yes. The 16-seat Sprinter on a timed loop is the usual solution; we plan the loop from your guest numbers and venue distance.

Do you cover venues outside Belfast?

Yes — anywhere in Ireland. Popular venues include the Causeway Coast, the Mournes, Fermanagh's lakes and Dublin/Wicklow.
