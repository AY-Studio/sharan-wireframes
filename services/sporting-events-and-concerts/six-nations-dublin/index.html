Match days

Belfast to the Aviva and back the same night

Ireland's Six Nations home games are played at the Aviva Stadium in Dublin in February and March. From Belfast that is about two hours each way, plus match-day traffic and road closures around Lansdowne Road — and a late finish that makes the return leg the hard part.

A chauffeur turns it into a door-to-door day: no parking, no last train, and nobody in the group has to stay sober against their will.

From £220 per vehicle each way confirm

The day

How a match day usually runs

  • Leave Belfast four to five hours before kick-off for an afternoon game confirm
  • Drop as close to the ground as the Garda traffic plan allows
  • Agreed collection point and a window rather than a fixed time
  • Home to Belfast the same night, or a hotel drop if you are staying over
  • Croke Park, the RDS and the Convention Centre all work the same way

FAQ

Six Nations transport questions

How long does it take to get from Belfast to the Aviva Stadium?

About 2 hours to Dublin, plus city traffic on a match day. We normally leave Belfast four to five hours before kick-off for an afternoon game confirm.

Where does the chauffeur drop off for the Aviva?

As close to Lansdowne Road as the match-day road closures allow — usually a short walk. The exact point depends on the Garda traffic plan for that fixture.

Can you wait and bring us home after the match?

Yes, and it is the most common booking. We agree a collection point and a window; standby time is charged hourly at the rate on your quote.

Is it cheaper than the train for a group?

For four or more it usually is, because the price is per vehicle rather than per person — and there is no walk to the station at either end at midnight.

Do you cover Croke Park and Windsor Park too?

Yes, and the SSE Arena, Ravenhill and race meetings. See sporting events and concerts.
