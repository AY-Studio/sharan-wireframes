About

About SHARAN Executive Transport

A Belfast chauffeur company with County Down roots, built on a simple idea: executive travel should be quiet, punctual and personal — and it should work the same way in Dublin, Derry or Donegal as it does in Belfast.

Who we are

Driven by excellence

SHARAN provides chauffeur-driven transport for business and private clients across the island of Ireland: corporate transfers and accounts, airport meet-and-greet at Belfast and Dublin, private tours of the Causeway Coast and beyond, golf transport, cruise excursions and event travel. We operate a Mercedes-Benz fleet from S-Class to sixteen-seat Sprinter, refreshed regularly, from our base on the Newtownards Road in East Belfast.

Founder statement — 2–3 sentences in the owner's words on why the company was started and what it stands for.

Our chauffeurs are the service. Clients name them in reviews — Ryan, Chris, Dearbhla — for arriving early, watching the traffic, keeping in touch and knowing where to stop on the coast. On corporate work they are discreet; on tours they are company.

How we work

What you can expect, every time

  • A written confirmation with price, chauffeur and vehicle — the price doesn't move on the day
  • Flight and traffic monitoring on every job
  • Contact from your chauffeur before collection
  • A valeted, late-model Mercedes-Benz
  • One point of contact from enquiry to invoice

Credentials

Licensed, insured, reviewed

  • Private-hire licensed in Northern Ireland and the Republic of Ireland licence numbers / operator details
  • Fully insured for hire and reward confirm
  • Chauffeurs vetted Access NI / equivalent
  • 5-star rated on Google, Tripadvisor and Trustpilot
  • Founded year; number vehicles, number chauffeurs

Where

Belfast base, County Down roots, all-Ireland reach

Our office is at City East Business Centre, 68–72 Newtownards Road, Belfast BT4 1GW. Our chauffeurs are largely from County Down — Castlewellan, Newcastle, the Mournes — which is why our local tours go where others don't. We're on the Belfast–Dublin road most days and across the island most weeks.
